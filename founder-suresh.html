About Suresh

Suresh Shrestha is the Chief Financial Officer and Founder of CodeGhar Nepal. As CFO, Suresh is responsible for all financial planning, budgeting, corporate governance, and compliance requirements of the company.

Suresh brings a disciplined, data-driven approach to managing CodeGhar's resources, ensuring that every investment delivers maximum return and that the company grows on a sustainable financial foundation.

He works closely with all department heads to align business decisions with financial realities — balancing ambition with accountability. His transparent reporting practices have earned the full trust of both internal stakeholders and external partners.

Suresh also manages client billing, contract negotiations, and vendor relationships — ensuring fair, professional, and clear financial terms across all engagements.
