Help Center & FAQ

Find answers to common questions about our services, process, and technology.

Business & Process

What is your typical project timeline?

For an MVP (Minimum Viable Product), we typically aim for a 6-10 week delivery lifecycle. Larger enterprise engineering projects or full digital transformations can range from 3 to 6 months depending on the technical complexity and integration requirements.

Do you offer post-launch support?

Yes, all our projects include a standard 30-day "Hyper-care" support period following deployment. For long-term partners, we offer tiered maintenance retainers that include proactive security patching, performance monitoring, and priority feature updates.

Where is your team located?

Our primary engineering headquarters is located in Kathmandu, Nepal. However, we operate as a remote-first foundry with specialized technical consultants distributed across South Asia and Europe ensures 24/7 responsiveness for global clients.

Technical FAQ

What technologies do you specialize in?

Our technical core is built on the modern JavaScript ecosystem (React, Next.js, Node.js), high-performance Python backends (FastAPI/Django), and robust cloud-native architectures utilizing AWS and Azure services.

Can you integrate with my existing systems?

Integration is one of our core strengths. We have extensive experience building secure API bridges and middleware layers to connect modern cloud platforms with legacy enterprise systems like SAP, Oracle, or custom on-premise ERPs.

How do you handle data security?

We implement "Security by Design." This includes SOC2-compliant workflows, OWASP Top 10 mitigation strategies, end-to-end data encryption (AES-256), and rigorous automated vulnerability scanning throughout the development lifecycle.
