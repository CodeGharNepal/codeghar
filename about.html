The Code Ghar Story

Founded in the heart of Kathmandu, Code Ghar emerged from a shared ambition by four engineers to elevate the standard of digital solutions in Nepal. We realized that while code is the language of technology, impact is the language of business.

Today, we operate as a specialized digital foundry, blending high-end software architecture with deep business intelligence. We partner with visionaries to transform legacy systems into modern platforms that are scalable, secure, and stunningly designed.
