The Code Ghar Story

Four of us started Code Ghar in Bhaktapur with a simple idea: Nepal deserves software that's as good as anything built abroad — without the agency price tag or the endless meetings.

Today we work with startups, local businesses, and anyone who needs a website, app, or internal tool built properly. We take old systems and rebuild them. We take rough ideas and turn them into working products. No fluff, no jargon — just solid engineering.

Our Philosophy

Good software shouldn't need a manual. We build things that are fast, easy to use, and solve a real problem — whether that's managing inventory, booking appointments, or running an online store.

We started as four friends who kept getting asked to build things for people. That turned into a company. We're still small, and that's intentional — you talk to the people actually building your product.
